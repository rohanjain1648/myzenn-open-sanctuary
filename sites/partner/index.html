How this demo works

Two separate websites
This planner and MyZenn are different sites with different agent networks. The planner's network names MyZenn's calm companion, and its links.json points that name at MyZenn's published network.
Your browser asks first
When you tell the planner that stress is getting in the way, it asks you before your browser contacts MyZenn. Then it loads MyZenn's network into this tab and runs it here, on your AI key.
Your data stays put
MyZenn's site never receives your messages, your key or your profile. Only your country can cross, and only if you allow it. The panel beside the chat lists every request this page made.

Read the files: the planner's network, its manifest, and MyZenn's site, which publishes the calm companion. Run the spec checks to see the runtime stop a denied origin, an invalid network, a cycle and an over-budget request.
